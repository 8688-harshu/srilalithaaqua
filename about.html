Our Roots & Purpose

Engineering Aquaculture from the Heart of Bhimavaram

Born in Andhra Pradesh's aquaculture heartland, Sri Lalitha Aqua was founded to solve recurring farm equipment breakdowns through rigorous mechanical craftsmanship and zero-friction fluid design.

Core Philosophy

Reliable Aeration Is Not A Luxury—It Is Harvest Insurance

When dissolved oxygen drops at 2:00 AM, farmers cannot afford a burnt coil or a stripped gear tooth. We build our equipment with double thermal margins, stainless marine hardware, and high-purity copper coils so operations run uninterrupted.

Milestones

The Path of Industrial Progress

From local machining workshops to statewide distribution, our journey reflects relentless dedication to quality.

2018 • Inception

Bhimavaram Production Center Established

Began manufacturing customized copper-wound submersible motors specifically tuned for local rural voltage fluctuations.

2020 • Breakthrough

Helical Bevel Gear System Deployed

Engineered the Gishnu and Lalitha heavy-duty gearbox line, eliminating routine oil seals failure and reducing electrical drag by 28%.

2023 • Statewide Reach

Dealer Hubs Across Godavari & Krishna Belts

Established direct dealer fulfillment points with guaranteed same-day mechanical spare deliveries during intensive pond culture periods.

2026 • Present

Next-Gen Hydrodynamics & Energy Savings

Deploying zero-friction aerator blade designs capable of sustaining high-density vannamei farming exceeding 80 pieces per square meter.
